How versus works

  • One player hosts and shares a 5-letter code (or invite link). The other joins. It’s a direct peer-to-peer connection — no account, no server of ours.
  • Both jars get the same pieces in the same order. You can watch your rival’s jar live.
  • Combos and big merges send stones to your rival’s jar: x2 combo = 1 stone, x3 = 2… and Wish Charm and up send more. Stones never merge and crumble after about 12 seconds.
  • Incoming stones land after a short warning — send an attack first and it cancels theirs.
  • Lose when your jar overflows. If time runs out, the higher score wins.
  • Every character’s ability works here too. Only characters you’ve unlocked in single player can be picked.
