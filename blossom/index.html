    How to play

    • Move your mouse (or drag) to aim, click / tap / Space to drop.
    • Match two of the same and they merge into the next one in the chain. Chain merges quickly for combo bonuses.
    • Don’t let the pile stay above the dotted line — the jar overflows.
    • Merges charge your character’s ability. Press Q or tap the ability button when it’s full.
    • Two Cherry Trees make a Full Bloom for a huge bonus.
    • Finish chapters of the visual novel and hit score goals to unlock more characters.
